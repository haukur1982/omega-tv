# Omega online — the vision

*Where we're taking Omega on the web and the phone, and why.*

## Why we're doing this

Omega has been on the air for 34 years, the longest-running Christian voice in Iceland. For most of that time, the way you met Omega was simple. You turned on the television, and there it was: the Word of God, worship, and prayer, sent into homes across the country, day and night, with no ads.

That doesn't change. The broadcast is the heart, and it stays.

But the way Iceland lives has changed. The phone is where the country sits now, every generation, all day. And right now, when someone reaches for their phone instead of the remote, Omega isn't really there. We have a beautiful website, but it works the way the television works: it speaks, you listen, and when you close it, it forgets you were ever there.

This is about changing that. Not replacing the broadcast. Adding to it. Taking 34 years of trust and finally giving Omega a presence on the phone that does what television never could.

## The purpose, first

Everything below serves one thing: to lift up the name of Jesus Christ.

We do that the way Omega always has, by carrying the teaching of men and women who are full of the Holy Spirit, who challenge, teach, and inspire. By praying for Iceland. And now, by building the things a phone makes possible that a television cannot: prayer that comes back to you, a daily word, courses and classes, a place to grow.

The goal is not traffic. It is not numbers on a screen. The goal is a place people love to return to, where they are fed, equipped, and lifted toward Jesus, and where they feel known.

## The honest truth about where we are

A real vision starts with the truth, so here it is plainly.

The website is beautiful and it is finished. There are sermons, articles, a prayer wall, a giving page, all made with care. But almost nobody is there yet. In the first days it was live, a few dozen real Icelanders visited. One person is on the email list. Not a single prayer has been submitted. Nothing yet points the cable audience to the web, and nothing yet brings new people in from the outside.

That sounds discouraging. It is not. It is the clearest possible diagnosis. We have a fire hose and a cup, and the hose is not pointed at the cup. The product is built. The audience pipe simply is not connected yet. That is a far better problem to have than the other way around.

## The one idea underneath all of it

Here is the idea that ties everything together: **give the ministry a memory.**

A television broadcast is something you catch. A home is something you return to. The difference is not more content or a prettier design. It is whether the place remembers you.

The line on our front page says, "We pray for Iceland every day." Today that is true of the channel. It is not yet true of the person. The whole vision is making that one sentence true for one believer at a time, until a widow in Selfoss can say out loud, "Omega knows me, and Omega is praying for me." The day she can say that, she is not visiting a website. She is home.

## Who it's for

The whole nation. Every generation. Phone first.

The people who have faithfully watched Omega on cable for decades are the anchor, and we serve them with everything we have. But this is also for the 30-year-old who has never once turned on the channel, for the young family, for the searching teenager. The phone is the one place all of them already are.

## The three moves

We get there in three moves, in this order.

**One: the Bridge.** Connect the cable audience to the web, and let Omega remember them. You are already watching Omega on television, and you trust it. We put one simple invitation on the screen: omega.is/tv. You go there on your phone, you land on a calm page, you tap one button, and the live broadcast plays. No sign-up, no wall. Then, gently, after you have watched, we offer to stay in touch by email. For the first time in 34 years, Omega has a direct relationship with the people it reaches, instead of a one-way signal into the dark.

**Two: Retain.** Give people a reason to come back, the kind only a ministry can give. The center of it is one thing: the prayer that comes back answered. Someone brings a burden to Omega in a hard season. Weeks later a message comes back, with their own words and the news that the prayer was heard. Around that sit a daily prayer that arrives each morning and a warm weekly letter. This is what turns an email address into a relationship, and a website into a home.

**Three: Reach.** Go out and find the generations who never turn on the cable. We take the most piercing moments from Omega's teaching and turn them into short, reverent clips that travel from phone to phone. A young person sends one to a friend who is falling apart at midnight, and that clip carries the real presence of God to someone who would never have found us otherwise. Omega already owns the machine that can make these at scale. We just point it outward.

Bridge brings your own people online. Retain holds them. Reach goes out for the rest of the nation.

## The promises we keep

A vision is also a set of promises about what we will never do. These protect the soul of the thing.

- **We never put the gospel behind a login.** Watching and reading stay open to everyone, always. No account, no password.
- **We never gamify prayer.** No points, no streaks, no leaderboards. Intercession is holy. The only numbers that belong are how many people prayed with you, and whether God answered.
- **We never nag.** A warm morning prayer and a weekly letter, sent like a pastor who loves you, not a marketer chasing you.
- **Reverence over reach.** We never trade the calm, dignified, ad-free feeling for noise or tricks, even when noise would spread further.
- **Warm and timeless, not trendy.** The look does not chase youth. It reads as quality to a 25-year-old and stays legible for a 75-year-old.
- **We handle tender things with care.** Prayers can carry real pain about health and family. We protect them, we ask consent honestly, and we never sell or expose anyone's information.
- **We steward the money.** Everything is built carefully and cheaply, with guards so nothing runs up a cost. Cable and donations remain the foundation. The web is added on top, never at their expense.

## What's already real

This is not only on paper. The foundation is built and tested.

- A real privacy policy and terms, so the whole thing is lawful and honest.
- The /tv landing page, where the cable audience will arrive, watch-first, with a gentle and lawful way to leave an email.
- The ability to see, for the first time, whether the cable audience crosses to the web, and which on-screen invitation moved them.
- The daily prayer on the front page, which used to be frozen on one date, now changes every day from a real pool the team can manage.

## What's next, and what we're waiting on

The next piece is the email layer: actually sending a person the weekly letter, the daily prayer, and one day the answered-prayer note. That is waiting on one technical step, verifying Omega's sending domain, which is already in motion.

After that comes the prayer-that-comes-back, and then the clips. Each one built carefully, shipped when it is ready, never rushed.

## What success looks like

Not likes. Not follower counts. Nothing to perform against.

Success is the first time Omega sends a real person the news that their prayer was answered. It is a grieving family getting a word from a real human instead of a form letter. It is a grandchild sending a 30-second clip of a sermon to a friend, and the gospel traveling one hop past the cable wall. It is a faithful older believer opening her inbox at 7 in the morning to a prayer, and feeling, for a moment, that she is not alone.

Lives touched. Doors opened. People fed and lifted toward Jesus, then sent back into their day. That is the whole thing. Everything else is just the plumbing that serves it.
