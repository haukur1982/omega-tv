# Faith Library — Seed Articles (first gathering pass)

Gathered 2026-06-20 by an AI gathering pass for the Omega faith-building article library.
Every URL below was fetched and confirmed to contain the actual readable text.
This is the import list: each item has a category, a rights flag, and notes.

## Rights legend
- **PD** = public domain, free to translate and publish (keep a source link anyway).
- **Likely PD (verify)** = almost certainly free, but confirm one thing first (see note).
- **CC (rewrite)** = Creative Commons text; safest to rewrite into our own original bio rather than republish verbatim.
- **NEEDS PERMISSION** = still under copyright. Do not publish without a yes.

> Note on the Voice of Healing magazine: the genuine scans live at teleiosministries.com (Revival Library / Tony Cauchi), mirror at ancientwells.org.au. They are image-only PDFs, so they need OCR before translation, and the early-50s issues carry a copyright line, so confirm the copyright was never renewed before treating as fully PD. Mid-1960s issues: treat with more caution.

## Notes for the build
- The cleanest, ready-now text sources are worldinvisible.com (Wigglesworth), ccel.org (Spurgeon, Murray), gutenberg.org (Müller, Taylor, Bounds, Bunyan), and wikisource.org (Woodworth-Etter). HTML or plain text, easy to pull straight into the inbox.
- archive.org "_djvu.txt" links (Paton, Slessor, and the details pages) give full plain text for excerpting.
- The Voice of Healing PDFs are image scans: OCR them before sending to BookForge, and confirm copyright non-renewal on the 1950s issues.
- A.A. Allen's own books and sermons are off limits without permission. The Voice of Healing magazine from his era is the safe path to that healing-revival material.
- Wikipedia bios are CC BY-SA: rewrite into our own original short bios rather than republishing.
