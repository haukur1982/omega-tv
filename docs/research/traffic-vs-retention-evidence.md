# Traffic vs retention — evidence pack (researched 2026-08-20)

Two-agent deep research supporting the thesis: paid traffic without capture
is nearly worthless; content rhythm + email relationship is what turns clicks
into a returning audience and donors. Basis for docs/fyrir-eirik.html and for
managing the Pikk/Birkir ad engagement (judge ads on cost-per-email, not
clicks). Only verified figures below; unverifiable ones flagged.

## Killer facts (all primary-verified)

1. Only 13% of first-time site visitors return within 30 days — 87% never
   come back. (Contentsquare Digital Experience Benchmark 2026; 99bn sessions)
2. First-time donor retention ~19%; repeat-donor retention 69%. Second gift
   triples the odds. (Fundraising Effectiveness Project, 2024; M+R 2026
   confirms independently: 24% vs 66% online)
3. Retention ladder: 1 gift 19% → 2 gifts 38% → 3–6 gifts 62% → 7+ gifts 87%.
   (FEP Q3 2025 via Bloomerang — note different YTD basis than #2)
4. Cost per donation: $61 search vs $1,040 TikTok; search ROAS $2.23/$1.
   (M+R Benchmarks 2025)
5. Facebook page engagement: 0.15% — ~1.5 interactions per 1,000 followers.
   (Socialinsider 2025; 25M posts. Note: the popular "organic reach %"
   figures do NOT trace to a primary source — use this instead.)
6. Email = 11% of nonprofit online revenue, $2.40/subscriber/year (+29% YoY),
   $54 per 1,000 fundraising emails. Monthly donors: 71% 12-mo retention,
   27% of online revenue. (M+R Benchmarks 2026)
7. One weekly cultivation email that asked for nothing: +80% engagement,
   +21% revenue. (NextAfter controlled test)
8. Multi-channel donors (e.g. cable + email) are worth 3x with 2x first-year
   retention. (NextAfter multi-channel study, 2020, n=119 orgs)
9. Half of weekly Christian-TV viewers have NO connection to any network;
   half of ministry donors have no connection to ministries they fund.
   48% of evangelicals use Christian media DAILY. (Infinity Concepts + Grey
   Matter, Feb 2026 — US data, analogy only, sample size undisclosed)
10. Nielsen: >75% of a returning series' premiere audience comes from
    on-channel promotion. The schedule is the best advertising. (Nielsen
    promo research — older study, directionally solid)

## The broadcaster-analogue goldmine: Public Media (M+R 2026)

Closest sector to a TV station, and 2025's standout:
- Email revenue +129.6% in one year; $309 per 1,000 emails (5.7x average)
- $4.61/subscriber/year; churn 6.2% (2nd lowest); lists +12.3%
- Sends 51 NEWSLETTERS per subscriber/year vs only 9 asks — the inverse of
  every other sector, and it wins. Membership = 98.8% of online revenue.
→ Omega's devotional-mailing-list instinct IS this model.

## Honest caveats (use these, stay credible)

- Paid ads DO work when judged as list-building (cost-per-email) and paired
  with capture; search already pays back $2.23/$1. Instruction to agency:
  optimize for signups + return visits, not reach.
- Cold start is real: organic search is shrinking (39% of nonprofit traffic,
  declining; 59% of sites lost traffic in 2025). Some paid spend is the
  legitimate on-ramp.
- OPEN RATES ARE BROKEN post-Apple MPP: opens nearly doubled (22.6%→40.5%)
  while clicks didn't move (Omeda, 2bn emails). M+R no longer publishes open
  rates at all. Judge email on clicks/revenue; opens only diagnose
  deliverability. (And note: Omeda Q2 2026 found 88.9% of raw click activity
  bot-driven — use unique clicks/conversions, directionally.)
- The famous "5-25x cheaper to retain" has a weak primary source. Use
  Reichheld & Sasser (HBR 1990): cutting defections 5% raised profits 25-85%.
- Faith-vertical email benchmarks: only current one is Constant Contact
  (Jan-Mar 2026): faith-based orgs have the HIGHEST open rate of 23
  industries at 41.8% (MPP-inflated, open-only). Mailchimp's oft-quoted
  Religion 27.6% row is Oct 2019, pre-MPP, deleted from their live page.
- Media-publisher newsletter medians (Omeda H1 2026, 1.95bn sends): open
  31.6%, click 1.16%, unsub 0.09%. Niche/enthusiast lists beat big general
  ones — engagement tracks audience type, not list size.

## Rhythm & habit findings (third agent, 2026-08-20 — all primary-verified)

The science behind a daily word + weekly letter cadence:

1. **78% of weekly podcast listeners consume a new episode of a favorite show
   within a day of release** (Cumulus/Signal Hill, Fall 2025, n=603). And 68%
   go back for episodes they missed. Audiences already live on release
   rhythms; a schedule is what they're waiting for, not an imposition.
2. **News subscribers who read ~10 days/month cancel at less than HALF the
   rate of 0-day readers** (Medill/Spiegel, Northwestern 2019). Frequency of
   return, not depth per visit, predicts staying. → the strongest single
   "rhythm creates retention" stat.
3. **Duolingo: a 7-day streak makes course completion 3.6x more likely**
   (Duolingo 2022); 15M people hold a 365-day streak (SEC 10-K FY2025).
   Daily rhythm compounds.
4. **Habit formation: median 66 days to automaticity (range 18–254), and
   missing one day does not materially harm it** (Lally et al. 2010, n=39 —
   cite honestly, small sample). Grace for a missed day is in the science.
5. **Pew 2026:** 71% of newsletter readers keep FEWER THAN FIVE newsletters;
   62% don't read most of what they receive. Being one of someone's five is
   the whole game — quality and identity beat volume.
6. **beehiiv 2026** (28bn emails): weekly cadence correlates with strongest
   subscriber growth (46.7% of creators send weekly); Tuesday best opens.
   Nonprofits have the top Mailchimp open rates (40%, Dec 2023 data).
7. Spotify Fan Study: podcast FOLLOWERS listen to 4x more episodes —
   subscription itself multiplies consumption. (→ email capture again.)
