# The audience system — from 33 years on cable to a nation online

**Date:** 2026-07-01
**Status:** Strategy. Sits ON TOP of the three moves (00–03). Those docs are the plumbing; this is what flows through it and on what rhythm.
**Owner:** Hawk, with Claude running most of the machine.

## The one sentence

Omega does not have an audience problem, it has a capture problem: the whole nation already knows the channel, 87% of Iceland is on Facebook, the website is finally worthy, and the only thing missing is the pipe that turns a viewer into a name we can reach tomorrow.

---

## The honest picture (researched 2026-07-01)

**Iceland is small, saturated, and spiritually unmoored.**

- ~400,000 people. Facebook: 318,600 users, 87% of the population (Dec 2025, NapoleonCat). Instagram: 222,400, ~61%. Messenger: ~70%. There is no "reaching Iceland" problem; the entire nation is behind two apps.
- The national church (þjóðkirkjan) has fallen from 92.6% membership in 1990 to 54.9% in August 2025. About 28% of Icelanders now register no religious affiliation.
- Read that decline correctly: most of those people did not become convinced atheists. They drifted. They are unchurched, not unspiritual. Nobody in Iceland is speaking to them daily in their own language about God.
- The active believer community (free churches: Hvítasunnukirkjan, Fíladelfía, Smárakirkja, Krossinn and the rest) is a few percent, scattered, many outside Reykjavík with no strong local church. They consume Christian content in **English** (Bethel, Elevation, podcasts) because almost nothing exists in Icelandic.

**Omega's position in that landscape is genuinely unique:**

1. **Channel 6 on Sjónvarp Símans**, plus Vodafone/Sýn and terrestrial. A default-lineup, channel-surfing audience that no online creator can buy. This is Omega's biggest "following" and it has never been pointed anywhere.
2. **33 years of trust.** Omega is *the* Christian channel of Iceland. Even people who joke about it know it. That position cannot be bought and no competitor is coming for it.
3. **The subtitle pipeline.** Icelandic subtitles on everything in ~20 minutes. Captioned clips, the single most expensive part of social video for everyone else, are nearly free here.
4. **The website, as of June 2026.** Daily word, daily prayer, prayer wall, VOD with clean metadata, articles, /tv bridge page, email capture with double opt-in, admin analytics. The machine exists. It shipped.
5. **Email that can actually send**, as of today (mail.omega.is verified on Resend).

The strategy is therefore not "build an audience from zero." It is: **connect the audience Omega already has to the machine Omega already built, and let the daily habits do the compounding.**

## Who we are reaching, in priority order

**A. The faithful core (55–75).** Cable watchers, donors, prayer partners. Already convinced. The job is not persuasion, it is *capture*: get them onto email so the ministry has a memory and a direct line that doesn't depend on a cable contract or an algorithm. They are on Facebook daily.

**B. Scattered believers (25–55).** In or around the small churches, or believing but churchless. Starved for Icelandic-language teaching, worship, and community. They are the growth engine because **they share**. A captioned clip in Icelandic is something they can finally send to their mother, their coworker, their group chat. Instagram + Facebook.

**C. The unmoored (the ~28%, skewing younger).** Will never walk into Fíladelfía. Will watch a 60-second honest clip at midnight, and might type a prayer request into a box that doesn't ask them to sign up. The prayer wall is the lowest-threshold door in Christendom. This group is the long game; we serve them by overflow, not by a separate strategy.

Priority: **A funds it, B grows it, C is the harvest.** Build for A and B; make sure nothing we build embarrasses or excludes C.

## The flywheel

One loop, every piece of content lives somewhere on it:

```
  BROADCAST / VOD (exists, runs anyway)
        │  clip it, card it  (subtitle pipeline makes this cheap)
        ▼
  SOCIAL (FB + IG daily presence)
        │  every post ends in ONE next step
        ▼
  OMEGA.IS (the home: watch, read, pray)
        │  every page offers ONE capture moment
        ▼
  EMAIL LIST (the only audience we own)
        │  daily word + weekly letter + prayer follow-up
        ▼
  RETURN & PARTICIPATION (prayer requests, amen, testimonies, replies)
        │  answered prayers and testimonies become new stories
        ▼
  NEW CONTENT ──────────────────────────► back to the top
```

The three moves map onto it: Bridge = broadcast→site, Retain = site→email→return, Reach = site→social→new people. This doc decides what actually flows: the content, the platforms, the rhythm.

## Content pillars (four, no more)

Everything posted anywhere belongs to one of these. If it doesn't, we don't post it.

**1. Orð dagsins — the daily heartbeat.**
One scripture + short reflection. Already on the site and in the DB, loaded ahead of time. Becomes: one branded card per day on FB + IG, and a short daily email for those who opt in. Same layout every day on purpose; recognizable at a glance, cheap to produce, compounding like a habit. This is the pillar that makes Omega *daily* instead of *sometimes*.

**2. Klippur — teaching that travels.**
60–90 second clips from sermons and shows, Icelandic captions burned in, 3 per week. The subtitle pipeline + Claude Design handoff (docs/social-clip-handoff.md) make this a batch job, not a creative grind. Every clip ends with one quiet line: "Allur þátturinn á omega.is." No hype, no jump cuts for their own sake; the moment carries it.

**3. Bæn — the participation pillar.**
This is the "be part of what we're doing" engine. Weekly post: "Hvað megum við biðja fyrir?" pointing at Bænatorg. The wall answers back ("við báðum fyrir þér"), which is the thing no other Icelandic media does. With permission, answered prayers become stories. Prayer is Omega's killer feature because it is the one thing an algorithm cannot fake and a visitor cannot get from Bethel in English.

**4. Fólkið og köllunin — the human pillar.**
Eiríkur, 33 years of the station, why Omega exists, Heimakirkja, behind the scenes. Roughly twice a month. This is what turns viewers into supporters; people fund a calling, not a schedule. Institutional warm voice (við/okkur), per the editorial rule, never first-person confession.

## Platforms: decisions, not options

**Facebook is the spine.** 87% of the nation, the donor base checks it daily, shares actually travel between generations there. Daily card, clips, prayer post, letter excerpt. If we do only one platform well, it is this one.

**Instagram rides along free.** Same assets crossposted via Meta Business Suite in the same sitting (card → feed, clip → Reels). Reaches segment B without a second production motion.

**YouTube is the archive and the search engine.** Full episodes uploaded (the VOD already has clean titles/descriptions from our metadata pipeline), clips doubled as Shorts. Icelanders searching "prédikun" or a speaker's name should land on Omega. Weekly upload, batch job.

**On-air is the forgotten platform and the biggest one.** End cards and lower-thirds on cable pointing to exactly two URLs: **omega.is/tv** (watch more, leave your email) and **omega.is/baen** (prayer). The Bridge plan (01) already specifies this; it is the highest-leverage single act in this whole document because it converts the audience we already have.

**TikTok: not yet.** Only after the rhythm above survives eight consecutive weeks. Starting a platform we can't sustain costs credibility with exactly the generation we'd be trying to reach. The clips will be sitting ready when we flip it on.

**Deliberately skipped:** X/Twitter (dead in Iceland for this audience), LinkedIn (wrong room), Snapchat (wrong format for us).

## The weekly rhythm (built to survive a bad week)

The whole system is **one batch session per week** (about 90 minutes, most of it Claude-assisted) plus automated dailies. Nothing depends on daily creativity or daily willpower.

| When | What | Pillar | How it gets done |
|------|------|--------|------------------|
| Daily, 07:00 | Orð dagsins card on FB+IG (+ email to opt-ins) | 1 | Auto from DB; scheduled a week ahead in one sitting |
| Sunday 09:00 | **Sunnudagsbréfið** — the weekly letter | 4/all | Drafted Fri (Claude drafts, Hawk edits 10 min), sends via Resend |
| Tue / Thu / Sat | Clip post (FB+IG Reels, YT Short) | 2 | Cut in the weekly batch from that week's VOD |
| Wednesday | Prayer post → Bænatorg | 3 | Template + rotating verse; answered-prayer story when we have one |
| Sunday evening | "Í kvöld á Omega" featured sermon post | 2 | From the schedule, templated |
| Monthly | One human story (Eiríkur, the station, Heimakirkja) | 4 | Planned in the monthly review |

**The batch session (Fridays):** pick 3 clip moments from the week's episodes → pipeline cuts + captions them → schedule everything for the coming week in Meta Business Suite → draft Sunnudagsbréf → done. If a week collapses, the scheduled queue still posts; the letter can be two paragraphs; the system survives.

**Live is bonus, never obligation.** A weekly FB-live prayer moment is a beautiful later addition, after eight clean weeks, not before.

## Email: the audience we own

Everything else is rented land. Facebook can throttle reach tomorrow; the cable slot is a contract; **the email list is the only audience that is actually Omega's.** North-star metric: verified email addresses.

**Capture moments (each page offers exactly one):**
- Homepage + articles: newsletter box (built)
- /tv: "fáðu vikubréfið" (built)
- Prayer flow: optional "viltu heyra þegar við höfum beðið?" (Retain plan)
- Orð dagsins: "fáðu orð dagsins í póstinn" daily opt-in (small build, content already in DB)
- Later: course/campaign signups

**What they receive:**
- Welcome + double opt-in (built, live once from-address lands)
- Sunnudagsbréfið, weekly (send flow built; needs the cadence started)
- Orð dagsins daily (to build; it's a template over existing data)
- "Við báðum fyrir þér" prayer follow-up (Retain plan)

**Rules that protect it:** never gate content behind email (no-login-walls rule), double opt-in always, every mail from a person-warm address (postur@mail.omega.is, baen@mail.omega.is), Reply-To goes to a real read inbox, one-click unsubscribe (built). Deliverability is load-bearing; we treat the list as a pastoral trust, not a marketing asset.

## Participation: how people become part of it

Hawk's ask: "encourage people to post and be a part of what we're doing." The ladder, lowest threshold first:

1. **Amen / "ég bið með þér"** on prayer wall entries. One tap, no account (Retain plan).
2. **Send a prayer request.** No signup required, ever. The single lowest-threshold door for segment C.
3. **Reply to the letter.** Every Sunnudagsbréf ends with one real question ("Fyrir hverju megum við biðja hjá þér í þessari viku?"). Replies land in the read inbox. People answer real questions from real senders.
4. **Share a testimony.** Vitnisburður page exists; push quarterly: "Hefur Guð svarað bæn í þínu lífi?" Published ones (with permission) become pillar-3 content, closing the flywheel.
5. **Become a Heimakirkja member.** Sóknargjald membership, the deepest commitment; the page is live and the drive gets its own moment once the rhythm holds.

Participation is the difference between an audience and a congregation. The wall that answers back is the moat.

## The four numbers (and only these four)

Watched weekly, on one admin screen:

1. **Verified emails** (total + new this week) — the owned audience. North star.
2. **Prayer requests per week** — participation pulse.
3. **Site sessions arriving from social + /tv** — is the bridge carrying anyone.
4. **Sunnudagsbréf open rate** — relationship health.

Vanity metrics (follower counts, likes) get glanced at monthly, never optimized for. A thousand people who open a letter every Sunday beat ten thousand passive followers, for ministry and for funding alike.

## Guardrails (what this deliberately does not do)

- **No daily manual grind.** Anything requiring daily creative willpower is designed out. Batch + schedule + automate.
- **No platform sprawl.** Two platforms done daily beat five done badly. TikTok waits.
- **No login walls, ever.** Watching, reading, praying stay open. Email is invited, not extorted.
- **No hype voice.** The description-prompt rule applies everywhere: concrete, warm, honest, zero "kraftmikill." Omega's institutional voice, never a personal one.
- **No new build phases hiding inside "strategy."** The site is built. The remaining builds are small (daily-word email template, analytics panel additions). If a task looks like a rebuild, it's a smell.
